# PRD — Agency / Partner

## Basic Candidate
- Agency organization/account
- Membership/roles
- Manage assigned businesses
- Create/onboard business
- Basic branding readiness
- Business switching
- Billing ownership options foundation

## Advanced Candidate
Basic + white-label domains/branding/email, wholesale pricing, consolidated billing, commissions, partner analytics, delegated support, reusable templates/playbooks, partner API, marketplace/provider ecosystem.

## Rules
Agency scope هرگز به unrelated business دسترسی ندارد. Business ownership/management/billing relationships explicit باشند.

## Capability Level Gate
**Level هنوز انتخاب نشده است مگر Owner صریحاً انتخاب کند.** قبل از implementation Agent باید Basic و Advanced را با scope دقیق ارائه کند.

## Engineering Baseline
در هر Level تمام Foundation Rules مربوط به Security, tenancy, tests, observability, data ownership, migrations, contracts و audit رعایت می‌شوند.
