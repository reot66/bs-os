# PRD — Marketing, Loyalty & Analytics

## Marketing Basic Candidate
Segments ساده، transactional/promotional messaging hooks، campaign basics، consent-aware sending.

## Marketing Advanced Candidate
Automation journeys، cross-channel workflows، templates، attribution، advanced segmentation، experimentation.

## Loyalty Basic Candidate
Points/ledger, earn/redeem rules basics, customer balance/history.

## Loyalty Advanced Candidate
Tiers, rewards catalog, campaigns, referrals, expiry/rule engine, branch/channel rules.

## Analytics Basic Candidate
Operational/product dashboards از transactional/read models.

## Analytics Advanced Candidate
Aggregated models/warehouse، custom reports، attribution/cohort، scheduled reports و AI insights later.

## Capability Level Gate
**Level هنوز انتخاب نشده است مگر Owner صریحاً انتخاب کند.** قبل از implementation Agent باید Basic و Advanced را با scope دقیق ارائه کند.

## Engineering Baseline
در هر Level تمام Foundation Rules مربوط به Security, tenancy, tests, observability, data ownership, migrations, contracts و audit رعایت می‌شوند.
